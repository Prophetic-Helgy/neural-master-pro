## EN post

Your AI track is singing. Now it subtitles itself. 🎤

v2.6 of Neural Master Pro adds karaoke subtitles to the video export — with a fully offline twist:

→ NEW Karaoke subtitles: a bundled Whisper model recognizes your lyrics word-by-word — on your machine. No cloud, no upload, no API key. Edit the lines, pick karaoke (words light up in time) or plain subtitles, and they are burned into the clip. The matching .srt downloads next to the video.
→ NEW flicker-free Pexels export: every exported frame is faithful now — no black flashes, no stale frames between clip cuts.
→ One-click mastering: 10 platform presets (Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok/Shorts, YouTube…), honest BS.1770-4 metrics — LUFS, true peak, LRA — in a Before/After table
→ Pro realtime mixer with 10 VST-class modules: parametric EQ, compressor, gate, transient shaper, de-esser, tape, air, bitcrusher, widener + MONO, phaser, flanger, tremolo
→ Stem Studio (Bass / Vocal / Mid / Side solo + WAV ZIP export) and Vocal Align (WSOLA dub snapping, pitch preserved)
→ 100% offline: your audio never leaves your device. No cloud, no account, no watermark
→ WAV 16/24/32f, MP3, FLAC, AAC + batch mode + 9 languages

The Whisper model ships inside the app (~250 MB installer) — that is the price of ASR that truly never calls home. Even your lyrics stay private.

vs CapCut / VEED subtitles: no upload, no subscription, works on a plane.
vs Ozone 12: no subscription, no DAW required — free.
vs LANDR / CloudBounce: your audio is never uploaded — at all.

Free for now, for personal use.
